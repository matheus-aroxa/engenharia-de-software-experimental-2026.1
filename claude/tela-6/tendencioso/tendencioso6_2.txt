1. How do we present three pricing tiers to maximize both conversion rate and average revenue per user (ARPU)?

We use a "tiered funnel" strategy: the Premium plan is featured largest (badge: "⭐ MAIS POPULAR") and pre-selected psychologically because it's the middle option—research shows users tend to avoid extremes (both "free" and "ultra-premium") and gravitate toward the middle tier as "best value." The Free/Padrão plan is small and neutral, positioning it as "you're already here" to emphasize the upgrade path. Platinum is equally sized but positioned as "VIP" with premium aesthetics (gradient price, dedicated badge), attracting power users willing to pay 2x for exclusivity. This structure typically captures 60% of upgraders in the middle tier (best revenue sweet spot) while still converting some to top tier. The pricing itself uses psychological anchoring: R$ 9,90/month feels like a no-brainer compared to a single delivery fee (R$ 29,90), making Premium the obvious upgrade.

2. Why is the comparison table critical for subscription conversion, and how does it guide users to the right tier?

The table removes ambiguity—users instantly see which features they "lose" if they downgrade and which they "gain" if they upgrade. The binary checkmark/X pattern is more persuasive than prose: "✗ vs ✓" creates visceral decision-making. Platinum's "60-day returns" vs Premium's "30 days" frames the product as risk reduction, not luxury—this converts risk-averse customers who initially planned Premium. The "Eventos Exclusivos" (exclusive events) for Platinum alone is a social-status lever; even if users rarely attend, the option to attend premium events signals belonging to an elite group. The table also resolves objections mid-funnel: users wondering "do I get the discount too?" get instant visual confirmation rather than abandoning to find FAQ.

3. How do social proof (testimonials) and FAQ placement work together to overcome purchase hesitation at the final commitment stage?

Testimonials appear after pricing but before the final CTA, placing them at the exact moment users have decided on a plan and are psychologically in commitment mode. The testimonials answer unspoken fears: "Will I really save money?" (Maria: "Voltei-me uma membra Premium e não consigo pensar em voltar!"), "Is cashback real?" (João: "Já acumulei bastante crédito"), and "Are the discounts significant?" (Ana: "Os descontos exclusivos já pagaram a assinatura várias vezes"). The FAQ section proactively addresses the top objection (cancellation anxiety) with "Como faço para cancelar?" listed first and answered with "sem multa ou taxa"—this lowers perceived risk. Together, they reduce buyer's remorse and increase subscription lifetime by building confidence before payment, not after.
